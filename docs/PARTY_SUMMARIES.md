# Customer and supplier financial summaries

Desktop reference: `lami216/offline-conta` commit `1b4951947a82ca9da21337c757fe7afdd2329403`, `app/party-metrics.ts`, `app/party-balance.ts`, `PartyAggregateMetrics`, `Parties`, and `PartyPage` in `app/conta-app.tsx`; `tests/party-financial-metrics.test.mjs`.

Goal: see the current two debt directions and historical trade/cash totals for a party category or one account, without confusing invoice amounts, invoice credit, later cash movements and current debt.

Interaction plan: retain the bounded directory and search. Replace its large net-balance banner with two compact current-debt cells; place four historical metrics below the directory in one shared panel. Within an account, show identity, the two current debt directions, then the four historical metrics before the existing movement history. Use two columns on phones, shared subdued borders, full semantic tint only for debt cells, normal text/amount colors, and explicit all-time scope. Every metric opens a bounded source list; a record still opens read-only detail before its existing source/edit actions. No invoice creation or settlement action is added to party pages.

Rules: aggregate only active parties of the selected category; searching and history date filters do not change these all-time totals. A directly opened archived account retains its own history. Customer trade = posted sales minus represented legacy returns; customer gross profit uses saved historical profit, never today's product cost. Supplier trade = posted purchases; purchase count counts invoices, not lines. Cash in includes the paid part of sales plus separate receipts; cash out includes paid purchases plus separate payments. Credit invoices create no cash until actually paid. Exclude cancelled/reversed documents and reversal audit entries. Current debt is computed separately per party, retaining both positive directions without netting different parties. The mobile supplier balance has the opposite stored sign to desktop party net; normalize it only for presentation.

Inputs/storage: read the existing parties, records, stored profit, balances and opening balances. No writes, migrations, backup changes or recalculation of stored balances. No change to invoice accounting, activation, user sessions or source navigation. Summary permissions follow `customers.view` / `suppliers.view` as desktop bootstrap does; source lists expose party-attributed amounts, not raw bank movement data. Invoice detail retains its existing permission gate.

Edge cases: zero/empty data, no search matches, archived parties, reverse customer/supplier balances, cash vs credit, later receipt/payment in either party direction, cancelled and reversal entries, missing historical profit, negative profit, cent rounding, long names, 100-million amounts and large histories. Source lists keep the existing 50-row pages and bounded scroll.

Validation: compare accounting fixtures against the fetched desktop pure functions; test Arabic/French, light/dark, narrow and wide layouts, metric sources, live cash changes, reload persistence, role restrictions and large histories. Verified results are recorded with the delivery evidence.

Local results: 80 aggregate comparisons against the unchanged fetched desktop functions over 10,000 mixed records passed. Sixteen narrow-screen captures cover both party types at directory/account scope in both languages and appearances; 13 source checks, all-time search scope and 100-million amounts passed. The 10,000-record aggregate completed in 9 ms in the local domain check.
