# Reviewed device entry

The owner creates organization accounts and reviews every new Android/Windows device. A new eight-digit phone identifier requests activation of this installation only; it does not create an organization. Existing valid local installations keep their normal local login, accounting and immutable license clock.

The entry screen has one identifier/password form, one consent checkbox, one main action, privacy and Arabic/French. There is no manual license import or activation contact page in the customer APK. The visual language uses the seller application's navy/gold colors and Arabic font, readable labels and 48px touch targets. Pending device approval explains its next action instead of presenting a purchase or activation link.

Local status loading does not disable editing. Late status responses must not overwrite a login attempt or entered draft. Mutating operations have visible progress and preserve recoverable input. Organization pending replies have no session, context, financial data or admission authority. The user explicitly signs in again after owner approval; no password is persisted or replayed automatically.

Administrative conversion links an already proven installation/key to an owner-selected organization/member. Local accounting is preserved. Moving accounting into a central ledger needs the owner's separate financial migration decision and a verified reconciliation plan; it is not inferred from linking a device.

Acceptance: Arabic/French, small viewports, keyboard/touch usability, delayed/lost status callbacks, unchanged old local login, phone request/approval, organization pending/retry/approval, denial from an older automatic-admission service, and absence of old manual/contact activation controls.
